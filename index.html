🔴 PROBLEM STATEMENT

38.1 million Nigerians (36.8% of adults) remain financially excluded — unable to access formal banking, credit, or savings tools due to:

  • Poor internet connectivity in rural and peri-urban communities
  • Lack of smartphone devices (feature phone penetration remains dominant in Ogun, Oyo, Kwara)
  • No formal collateral or credit history to access microloans
  • Existing fintech platforms (OPay, PalmPay, Kuda) all require Android smartphones + active internet
  • Informal savings systems (ajo/esusu) are unprotected against fraud, default, and theft

Core Features Built:
  • Peer-to-Peer USSD Transfers — Send money via phone number, no bank account needed
  • Digitized Ajo/Esusu Cooperative Savings — Groups contribute into rotating digital pools with tamper-evident audit trail
  • Deterministic Credit Scoring Engine — Computes behavioral credit scores from savings consistency, repayment history, and peer-vouching signals using zero third-party dependencies
  • Agency Banking Dashboard — Web interface for POS agents to monitor member transactions and generate audit reports
  • SMS Fallback Notifications — All confirmations delivered via SMS for areas with USSD instability

📐 CREDIT SCORING ENGINE — MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION
MeshPay Credit Score (MCS) — Deterministic Behavioral Scoring Algorithm MCS(u) = w₁·S(u) + w₂·R(u) + w₃·P(u) + w₄·F(u) Where: S(u) = Savings Consistency Score — normalized savings frequency over 90-day window R(u) = Repayment History Score — ratio of on-time repayments to total loan obligations P(u) = Peer Vouching Signal — weighted endorsement score from cooperative members F(u) = Transaction Frequency Score — volume and regularity of micro-payment activity Weights: w₁=0.30 w₂=0.35 w₃=0.20 w₄=0.15 (sum = 1.00) Output: MCS ∈ [0, 850] — CBN-aligned credit score range Latency: 0.018ms per evaluation | Zero third-party API dependencies
